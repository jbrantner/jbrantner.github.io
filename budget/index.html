Import from your bank

Download your transactions as a CSV file from your bank's website or app, then pick it here. Duplicates are skipped, so overlapping downloads are fine.

Spots your monthly bills in 2–3 months of statements (PDF or CSV). It only reads them: nothing is imported.

Auto-categorize rules

When a description contains the text, it goes in that category. Rules are also learned when you pick a category for an imported transaction.

Bank sync

Not connected. Automatic sync needs a bank-data service (SimpleFIN or Plaid) and a small server; the app is set up so that can be added as another source later.

Find your bills

Pick 2–3 months of bank statements and the app looks for what comes out every month (rent, phone, subscriptions) and the day it usually hits.

  • Chase app: open your account, go to Statements, open each of the last 2–3 months and save the PDF to Files.
  • chase.com: or download your account activity as a CSV.

The files are read right here on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere or saved, and nothing is added to your spending.
