Ember

XP this week

Each bar drains over its own rhythm. Tap Done to refill it. A habit's level counts the days you kept it in rhythm (10 days a level), so it means the same for easy and hard things. Your pet gets XP for those days. If a bar sits empty for more than a day, that habit slips a day each day. Your pet keeps the XP it already has.

Tap Chip every time you make any progress, big or small. Mash it for a lot. Each chip is 2 XP for your pet. The size is only a guess: if the bar fills and it isn't done, tap Need more. Tap Finished whenever it's really done (+20 XP).

Something set you back? Tap Setback (or mash it) to take chips off. It never costs XP. Big things can have steps: the to-do's chips are split evenly between them, each step gets its own little bar, and Chip goes to the next step. A finished step is +10 XP.

Spaces are things like your room that can get messy again. Split one into spots and tap a spot when it's tidy. If something makes a spot messy again, tap it again. The bar shows how much of the space is tidy right now. Tidying a spot is 2 XP (once a day per spot), and getting the whole space to 100% is +20 XP once a day. Mess never costs XP.

Goals are big things you chip away at over weeks. Chips on a to-do that belongs to a goal count for the goal too. You can also chip a goal directly.
