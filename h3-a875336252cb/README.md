# H3 Deck

A phone front end for MiniMax H3 on ComfyUI, running on both PCs (Render and Main) over Tailscale, in the Render Deck look.
App: https://jbrantner.github.io/h3-a875336252cb/ (add it to the home screen). The whole app is `index.html`; no build step.

## What it does
- **🛟 Films and Library kept on the PCs** (Jordan 2026-11-11, after the phone cleared them): every save also writes `h3deck_films.json` (films, Library, per-film forms and casts, their media entries and upload names) to each PC's ComfyUI user files, never an empty one, and only after the app has looked for a copy. When the app starts with no films or no Library, it brings back the newest copy and fetches the pictures back from the PC's `input/h3deck` folder. With no copy, the films are rebuilt once from the renders (each take knows its film; options and redos go under their clip, carry-ons after, lead-ins before). Test: tests/t_rescue.js.
- **↶ Undo / ↷ Redo** (top bar; Jordan 2026-11-09): one history for the whole app: Shot box words (a run of typing is one step), form settings per film, cast, 📚 Library, takes' words, takes taken off the list, and the 🎞 Films timelines and prompt blocks (the timeline's own ↶ is the same undo). Only taps and typing make steps; changes the app makes itself (renders finishing, the playhead loading a clip's words, opening another film) are folded in quietly. Takes whose videos were deleted on the PCs (✓ Keep only this) can't come back. History lasts until the app is closed. Ctrl/⌘+Z and Shift+Ctrl/⌘+Z outside text boxes. Test: tests/t_undo.js.
- **Model**: every MiniMax H3 family the PCs have (DaSiWa Hybrid V2, H3 ErosMax, official Reference and First/last frame). **Turbo** uses the family's own turbo file, or the base file + the official turbo LoRA (4 steps for reference models, 8 for FL2V); **Regular** is 20 steps. Optional MiniMax style LoRA with strength and activation words.
- **First / last frame** toggle (last frame its own toggle) and **References** toggle: up to 9 pictures, 3 videos (🔊 with their sound, ⏱ follow in time) and 3 sounds (🎤 sample or 🎵 soundtrack the clip follows), each on or off. Slots show the `<Picture n>` / `<Video n>` / `<Audio n>` name MiniMax will use. References go through MMH3 Fit References (the low-memory node) when the PC has it.
- **Canvas**: aspect chips, a resolution slider (kept inside MiniMax's 768×1344 cap, multiples of 32) and preset sizes.
- **Long clips**: anything over the "longest piece" (5 s by default, ⚙ More) is split into near-even pieces on the 17k+5 frame grid (6 s → 3 s + 2.8 s, not 5 + 1). Pieces run in order on one PC and are joined with **H3 Motion Context** (last 22 frames + 1 s of sound of the previous piece's saved latent pinned at the head of the next, trimmed after decoding). Timed beats in the prompt (`[0s-3s] …`, `At 4s, …`) are moved into the piece they fall in and re-timed; a cut lands on a beat change when one is close. Each piece's prompt can be edited. When all pieces are done the app queues ComfyUI's Concatenate Video (no re-encode) for one file.
- **Both PCs**: each take goes to the PC that will be free first (Main counts 1.26× slower). A split clip needs H3 Motion Context on that PC. Takes run in ComfyUI's own queue, so they carry on with the app closed; joining happens next time the app is open.
- **🪄 Smooth a video** (Frames and references): pick a video and where the window starts; the Length slider sets the window (up to 15 s, 5 s → 124 frames). The video goes to the PC as it is and the frames are taken there with core nodes (LoadVideo → Video Slice → GetVideoComponents → ImageFromBatch). Every 2nd/4th/8th frame plus the last is pinned with its own MiniMaxH3AddGuide at its frame, and the clip's sound from frame 0 when it has sound; MiniMax paints the frames between. Keyframes have no strength setting: MiniMax re-injects them every step and never denoises them, so they are always at full strength. The frame rate is read from the file's MP4/MOV boxes, and a 30 fps clip uses the nearest source frame for each 24 fps frame. Each pinned frame costs its own tokens (32 × (w/32)·(h/32) for 5 s at every 4th frame). One window per take for now; sliding windows for long clips are the next step.
- **📐 Even out the spacing** (on by default inside 🪄 Smooth): the phone seeks through the window (each output frame's source frame, 192 px wide) and block-matches ~30 textured patches from frame to frame (coarse ±12 px, exact, then sub-pixel; a small cost per pixel moved so plain edges read as still), taking the 70th-percentile move as that frame's step. A jump is a step over 2.5× its neighbours' median. The eased plan smooths the steps with a Gaussian (Easing slider × 6 frames, same total distance) and each pinned slot takes the source frame nearest where the eased motion says it should be (so a source frame can move to another output frame); a slot with no close source frame (inside a jump) is left unpinned for MiniMax to paint. The chart shows the steps (red = jump), the eased line, and the pinned (green), re-timed (blue) and unpinned (✕) slots. Render waits until the measuring is done; if the phone can't read the video, it pins the plain grid.
- **🎬 Punch** (slider under Easing, off by default; Jordan 2026-11-02): animator spacing on top of the even-out. Steps are blurred into moves (at least a 2-frame Gaussian, so on-4s clips read as motion), split at holds (speed under 15% of the 90th percentile). Each move keeps its distance and length, but its speed curve is read on a warped clock (u^(1+0.8·punch), so the fast part comes later: a longer ease in and a quicker stop; only for moves of 48 frames or fewer, so a long pan isn't re-timed) and raised to the power 1+2·punch (slower slow parts, faster middle). Drift between moves is calmed toward a hold. The pins land on that curve and MiniMax paints the rest. `punchSpeeds()` in index.html.
- **🔁 Make it loop** (inside 🪄 Smooth; Jordan 2026-11-02): output frame o pins source frame (o + h) mod L (h = half the window), so the window's own wrap (last → first) lands in the middle of one MiniMax render and the render's ends are the window's continuous middle. Pins within seam/2 of the wrap are left out (Free frames slider, 4–32, default 12) and MiniMax paints the turn from the motion on both sides. Sound is off in loop mode. 📐 spacing and 🎬 Punch work in loop mode (PR after Jordan saw pops 2026-11-02): the measured steps are put in the swapped order (the wrap step takes its neighbours' average), planned there, mapped back to source frames, and pins near the seam are dropped (`planSpacing()`). `loopKeys()` is the plain-grid fallback; the graph is the normal smoothing graph with these key pairs.
- **⬆ Then regenerate bigger** (inside 🪄 Smooth, off by default; size 1.25× or 1.5×, short side capped at 768; Repaint Light/Medium/Full; Strength 0.4–1, default 0.8): the open copy of MiniMax's 2K regenerate ([Open-H3-Regenerate-2K](https://huggingface.co/Efficient-Large-Model/Open-H3-Regenerate-2K), ComfyUI port [father558 r128](https://huggingface.co/father558/H3-Open-Regenerate-2K-ComfyUI-r128)). When the smoothed clip is done, the app asks ComfyUI to unload MiniMax (`/free`) and queues a second job on the same PC: LoadVideo (the smoothed file) → GetVideoComponents → VAEEncode → `MinimaxH3LatentUpscaler3D` (LBH, target dimensions, bf16) → `LTXVConcatAVLatent` with the empty audio latent of `MiniMaxH3ImageToVideo` (prompt, new size, same length) → `SamplerCustomAdvanced` (FL2VA int8 + regenerate LoRA at Strength, BasicGuider CFG 1, euler, `ManualSigmas` Max detail `0.909375, 0.857143, 0.75, 0.6, 0.45, 0.3, 0.15, 0` / Medium (default) `0.8, 0.666667, 0.45, 0.2, 0` / Light `0.7, 0.5, 0.25, 0`; Medium + 0.8 won Render's settings sweep 2026-11-01) → VAEDecodeTiled → CreateVideo with the smoothed clip's sound. Like joining, it starts when the app is open. The smoothed clip stays in `t.smoothed`; the regenerated one becomes the take's video. Render 2026-11-01: 56 frames 512×768 → 768×1152 in 129 s, peak 86°C, so keep clips short there. Needs on the PC: `custom_nodes/Comfyui_Minimax_h3_latent_Upscaler`, `models/latent_upscale_models/minimax_h3_latent_upscaler_3d_conv_v1_bf16.safetensors`, `models/loras/minimax_h3_open_regenerate_2k_r128_comfyui_bf16.safetensors`, a MiniMax FL2VA model.
- **🪄⬆ Smooth & regenerate** (button on any finished take, Jordan 2026-11-02): fetches the take's video (the smoothed/original source, not a regenerated copy) from its PC onto the phone and loads it into 🪄 Smooth with the take's prompt (＋ parts joined), model, LoRAs, size (mp) and smoothing settings, with ⬆ Then regenerate bigger on. The take's reference pictures go to the regenerate step only (`f.smRefs` → `t.upImgs`, toggle **Use its reference pictures**, first 3; Ref2VA int8 + `MiniMaxH3ReferenceToVideo`); the smoothing itself stays without refs. Nothing runs until Render is pressed. The older direct sheet (`regenSheet`: same regenerate from the take's own video, joined clips cut to the 17k+5 grid) is still in the code.
- **🦴 Motion guide** (Frames and references; Jordan 2026-11-03): a skeleton or depth clip, usually from Mocap, that every frame follows through MiniMax's own Fun ControlNet (ModelPatchLoader with the MiniMax H3 union file from models/model_patches → MiniMaxH3FunControlNetApply on the model; Strength, and Lets go = end_percent, default 1.0 / 80%). Each piece reads its own stretch of the clip on the PC (LoadVideo → Video Slice from the piece's start minus its carried-over frames → GetVideoComponents); the node fits it to the take's size from the middle. The picture references still say who is in it. Mocap's 🦴 H3 Deck button puts the clip in the PC's ComfyUI input/mocap and leaves a note in that PC's user files (h3deck_inbox.json); H3 Deck picks it up when it opens or comes back to the front, keeps it on the phone, puts it in Motion guide and sets the take's shape and length to the clip's (the PC carries the hand-off because a home-screen app has its own storage). Under the slot, **From Mocap** lists every skeleton/depth clip from those notes plus any `mocap_<name>_<stamp>_<w>x<h>_<tenths>_<clip>.webm` in a PC's input folder (read from `/internal/files/input`, or LoadVideo's list), so the hand-off works even without the note. **Second guide** (2026-11-04, Jordan picked): an optional second clip, usually depth under a skeleton, gets its own MiniMaxH3FunControlNetApply chained onto the first (the node wraps any earlier block patch, so both steer; one ModelPatchLoader feeds both), default 0.4 letting go at 50%. Tapping a skeleton in From Mocap brings that capture's depth along as the second guide; each row's **2nd** button puts any clip there; a skeleton from another capture clears a mismatched one. The take estimate counts x1.5 instead of x1.3.
- Per take: live step progress and preview, ✕ Cancel, ⬇ Save, ➕ Add more (continues from the end), 🎲 New take, tap a piece to replay it or make it again from there, or 🎲 Takes from here: N new takes that keep the pieces before it and remake it onwards, carrying on from the earlier piece's saved ending (`ctxDirs[j]` = the folder holding piece j's ending; such takes join with Concatenate Video, not the folder-only helper join). Test: tests/t_branch.js.

- **🖼 Ref images for this shot** (under the Shot box, also in bare bones; Jordan 2026-11-12): pictures only this shot uses, never in the 📚 Library. Each is numbered with a chip (🖼 Ref Image 1, 2, …; also in the tag bar) that puts `<Ref Image k>` in the words, which show it as a chip. Sent as that picture's own `<Subject n>` (H3 way, "what is shown in <Picture n> is kept") or `<Picture n>` (plain); an optional "what it is" names it. They share MiniMax's 9 pictures with the characters: shot refs go first, but each character the words name keeps at least one picture (＋ stops at that limit, castTake drops any extra and its words stand in). ✕ takes its tag out of the words and renumbers the rest; a ➕ carry-on or a clip loaded without them starts with none. Takes keep `rk` on each picture. Test: tests/t_refimg.js.

- **🧾 Write it the H3 way** (Shot, on by default; Jordan 2026-11-04): sends the prompt in MiniMax's own structured format, the one its hosted H3-Context-IR rewriter produces and the open checkpoints were trained on ([Ref2VA guide](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/blob/main/docs/VIDEO_PROMPT_WRITING_GUIDE_ref_en.md), [base guide](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/blob/main/docs/VIDEO_PROMPT_WRITING_GUIDE_base_en.md)). With references: `subject_definitions` (pictures with the same words become one `<Subject n>`; a first frame on a hybrid model is `<Picture n> is the first frame of [Shot 1]`), `summary` with the task tags (`video continuation` on pieces 2+, `keyframe completion`, `reference generation`, `audio reuse`/`audio reference`), `retention_analysis` (Keep exactly / Mostly / Loosely = `fully_preserved` / `partially_preserved` / `weak_reference`; sounds: `reference` or `partially_copy`), `detailed_description` (style sentence, then `[Shot 1]` + the piece's prompt), `overall_soundscape` (empty = natural sound of the scene) and `non_diegetic_music` (empty = N/A). The FL2V model and text-only shots get the base format: the official alignment line for first/last frames, then `integrated_multimodal_description`. LoRA activation words go first. The form: Style, who/what each reference is with its keep level, sounds, music, a word count against the official 350 to 500, and 👁 What MiniMax gets (piece 1). Off = the prompt goes as typed (old takes too). `h3Prompt()` in index.html; test `tests/t_h3way.js`.

## Why H3 Motion Context
Checked 2026-11-01: the newer chaining packs (MiniMax H3 Extender, H3 Long Video, Motion Context Auto-Chain, H3 Continuum) all use the same idea, a 22-frame latent tail as the next clip's head. H3 Motion Context was already installed and tested on Render (2026-10-03) and is plain nodes the app can drive through the API.

## 👁 Touch up (touchup/)
A page of its own (the 👁 button in the header) for a clip you already have: put a picture into the eyes (e.g. a spiral), colour the lips, or change any part you name or tap. One ComfyUI job on the PC: LoadVideo → Video Slice → GetVideoComponents (≤1280 px) → masks (MediaPipe face mesh for real faces: whole iris circles sized by the mesh and cut by the eye openings, or the lips; SAM 3 video tracking for a word like "iris:2" or for taps on the first frame) → the Render Deck helper's `RenderDeckTouchUp` (picture warped onto each part's ellipse, steadied over a few frames, with the video's own shading, catchlights and fine texture kept on top; or a recolour that spares teeth and the open mouth) → CreateVideo with the clip's sound and frame rate → SaveVideo. "Try one frame" runs the same graph on a 0.15 s slice and saves a still.

## 🧍 Poses (keys/)
Pose keys on a timeline, opened as H3 Deck's 🧍 Poses tab (an iframe, so it hands its files straight to the Render form under the Films timeline).
- Poses come from Render Deck's 🤸 pose library (kind `posebook` in the helper's scenes store: a SAM 3D Body GLB + tweaks). ＋ New pose opens Render Deck's pose page over the tab.
- Every key is shown on one body (the first pose's GLB) with each pose's bone turns copied onto it, so ▶ can play Stepped (hold each key) or Eased (slerp bone by bone). 🎥 Still = one shot camera; 🎥 Moving = a view per key, eased between them.
- 🎬 Use in Render makes, from the shot camera at the clip's shape (long side 832): an OpenPose skeleton clip and a depth clip (24 fps, the timeline's length) for 🦴 Motion guide, and one grey-mannequin picture per key. In the Render tab those pictures are references with `pose:{t}`; the H3-way prompt makes each a pose subject (`attribute_transfer` onto the person) and says "At MM:SS.mmm, … is in the pose of <Subject n> from <Picture k>" with times counted from each piece's own start.
- Test: `SAM_GLB=a.glb SAM_GLB2=b.glb RIG_DEPS=<dir with three-0.160.0/package> NODE_PATH=$(npm root -g) node tests/t_posekeys.js`

## Screens (2026-11-06)

There are two tabs. 🎞 Films is the main screen. 🧍 Poses is the second.

On 🎞 Films, from the top down:
- the film app (`film/?embed=1`), in a frame that grows to fit its content;
- below it, the whole Render form: Shot, inputs, model, More, and the Render button;
- below that, the list of takes, with every take button.

How it behaves:
- With no film open, Render makes one-off takes, just as the old 🎬 Render tab did.
- With a film open, the first take goes onto that film's timeline. ➕ Carry on and ↻ Redo set things up from the timeline.
- Scrubbing loads the form with the take and the words under the playhead. This stops once you change something, and ↺ Follow the clip again turns it back on.
- `#take=<id>` links and ✏️ Its take scroll to that take in the list.

## ⬆ Upscale track (2026-11-11)
In 🎞 Films (tools row, or a track's menu). Runs in three steps and remembers where it is with the film (`f.upRun`), so the app can be closed:

## 🔎 Real-PC test (2026-11-14)
A test with real jobs on both PCs and Jordan's real films (loaded read-only) found and fixed:
- **⚡ Films was slow with real data**: `takeOf` looked through all 300+ takes thousands of times a draw. Opening a 21-clip shot took 7 s on a PC (over 20 s at phone speed) and scrubbing ran ~107 ms a frame. Takes are now found from a map that lasts one piece of work (`takeIdx()`), and `rootOf` / `versionsOf` are worked out once per piece of work: 0.16 s to open, ~6 ms a scrub frame.
- **🧷 No job twice**: Render rendered two takes twice (13 min of GPU) when the phone went to the background just as it sent them: the hidden copy started from a save without the job's id and sent it again. `queueGraph` now uses the same job if it's already waiting or running on that PC (`sameJob()`: same graph = same take, piece, seed and settings).
- **⇄ Joins and 🧵 seams move to a free PC** too, once that PC sees every clip in them (a HEAD of each through Dropbox) and has the seams' model. Main sat idle 20+ min while a seams job waited behind another on Render.
- **🌙 No reload for nothing**: Render opened the hidden copy 59 times in 13 h, often for 7-30 s while Jordan switched apps, and each time the app reloaded on her return to take over its copy. When the hidden copy changed nothing (`sameRun()`), the app now just carries on.
- **The takes copy on the PCs** is never overwritten with an empty list (unless 🧹 Start fresh meant it).
- **Sheets after a send**: ⇿ Fill, ✏️ Edit, 🎭 Faces, ⏪ Lead in and the others re-enabled their button and closed "the sheet" when the send came back, a few seconds later; if another sheet had been opened meanwhile that threw an error and closed the wrong sheet. They now leave a sheet that isn't theirs alone.
