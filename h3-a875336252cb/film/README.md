# 🎬 H3 Film

A whole film with MiniMax H3, from the phone: https://jbrantner.github.io/h3-a875336252cb/film/ (also the 🎬 button in H3 Deck). The whole app is `index.html`; no build step. It talks straight to each PC's ComfyUI like H3 Deck, shares its pictures store (IndexedDB `h3deck`) and starts from its PC list. Its own state is localStorage `h3film`.

- **Film** › **scenes** › **cuts** › **parts**. A cut longer than "Longest part" (5 s) is split into near-even parts on the 17k+5 grid (H3 Deck's `plan()`).
- **🎭 Cast**: each character has a name, a description and pictures ranked most to least important (◀ ▶ to re-rank, ✏️ a note like "her face up close"). In the description `{2}` (or `<Picture 2>`) means that character's own 2nd picture.
- **Cast tiles** (Jordan 2026-11-02): characters fold into tiles of their #1 picture (tap to open, tap the name to fold; new characters start open).
- **🏷 Name chips** (Jordan 2026-11-02): above the action, each part and the redo box, one chip per character in the cut writes their name at the cursor. MiniMax has no character tags (only <Picture n>); the cast line binds names to pictures, so the name is the tag.
- **👗 Outfits** (Jordan 2026-11-02): each cast card has tabs Base / 👗 outfits / ＋ Outfit. An outfit has a name, words for what she wears (`{n}` = the outfit's nth picture) and its own ranked pictures. In a cut, chips under each character pick her outfit (`cut.outfit[charId]`): MiniMax then gets the outfit's pictures (her base ones while the outfit has none), her description loses its `{n}` sentences, and the prompt adds "Instead of the clothes above, in this cut <name> is wearing …". 🪄 in an outfit: 👗 **Dress her** (Qwen Edit from one of her base pictures: same face/hair/body, new clothes, full body) / 📚 Reference set from an outfit picture / ✏️ Edit; results added go into the outfit.
- **🪄 Make pictures** on each cast card (Jordan 2026-11-02): ✨ **From words** = Z-Image Turbo (8 steps, res_multistep, AuraFlow shift 3, 896×1152, one job per try; **Variety** Same/Some/Lots = each try gets its own wording (Some: light, expression, framing; Lots: also two face details), because Turbo gives near-identical faces from any seed. Running the first steps with no prompt was tried and painted white stripes and busy rooms over her (2026-11-02), so the prompt always leads) makes a realistic front photo from her description; 🖼 **From a picture** = Qwen-Image-Edit (newest of 2511/2509, its Lightning LoRA, AuraFlow 3 + CFGNorm, TextEncodeQwenImageEditPlus with the picture as image1, a new 896×1152 page) redraws any picture as a clean front reference; ✏️ **Edit** = Qwen on the picture itself (its own latent, same framing) with only the change you write ("change her hair to green") + "keep everything else the same"; 📚 **Reference set** = the same Qwen recipe, one job per ticked angle (face, ¾, profile, full body, smile, back, walking, laugh) from the picture you pick. Her description goes in (minus sentences about her own `{n}` pictures) plus any extra words. Jobs go into that PC's Render Deck helper line (`/renderdeck/farm/add`, pinned to the PC; Qwen = whichever PC has it, i.e. Render), which keeps them on disk and hands them to ComfyUI one at a time, so closing the app or a ComfyUI crash/restart doesn't lose them (the line resends a lost job once; Render crashed mid-Qwen on 2026-11-02 and wiped 7 waiting jobs from ComfyUI's in-memory queue). The app reads finished/failed ones back from the helper's record when it opens; ✕ / Cancel the rest take them out of the line. An older helper, or a PC the helper doesn't list, gets them straight into ComfyUI's queue as before; results wait on her card under "New pictures": #1 makes one her top picture, ＋ / Add all puts them after hers (angles in order, each with a note like "a side profile").
- **Picture sharing** (`alloc()`): a cut has up to 9 picture slots (🎛 Look › Pictures per cut). Characters load in the cut's order; slots are dealt by rank (everyone's #1, then everyone's #2…), the scene's place pictures get 1 slot after them. − / + on a character fixes their count for that cut. Numbering follows the order, so character 2's pictures start right after character 1's. The cast text (`castText()`) names each one's pictures, adds the notes, and renumbers `{n}` in the description; a sentence about a picture the cut leaves out is dropped.
- **Parts' prompts**: the cut's action is shared out over the parts (timed beats `[0s-3s]` to the part they fall in, else whole sentences); each part's text can be edited. When a part is sent, the app adds the continuity line plus "Already happened, and is not repeated: <the part before>. Now: …", so part 2 doesn't replay part 1's action. Every prompt = LoRA words + style (realistic live-action by default) + cast + place + action.
- **One PC per cut** (Jordan 2026-11-02): a cut's takes all go to the PC picked when it starts (each part carries on from the picked take's saved latent there); different cuts are spread over the PCs by how busy they are. Main can be switched off in ⚙.
- **✂ Cutting Room**: every part with takes, from every film. N takes of part 1 are queued; tap one to watch it after the last 3 s of the parts already picked (or from the start); ✓ Use queues N takes of the next part, each carried on by H3 Motion Context (LoadLatent of the picked take's folder, `clip_index` = part number). Picking the last part queues ComfyUI's Concatenate Video for the cut. Renders run in ComfyUI's own queue, so the app can be closed; it picks up what finished when it opens. ↺ Pick another take on a finished part makes the later parts again.
- **✂ Redo a stretch** of a finished cut (From/To sliders or a Part chip): one job on the cut's PC. MiniMax renders a 17k+5 window starting at frame a (or 12 frames before it) and ending at b (or 12 after), with the cut's frames pinned by `MiniMaxH3AddGuide` (a and b always; with "Use the frames around it" every 2nd frame of the context too) and the cut's sound pinned for lip sync; then `ImageFromBatch` + `ImageBatch` splice cut[0..a] + new frames between a and b + cut[b..], with the cut's own sound. Takes go to the Cutting Room; ✓ Use makes it the cut (↩ Undo keeps the one before).

Test: `tests/t_film.js` (fake Render and Main in the browser): picture sharing and renumbering, part prompts, one PC per cut and cuts spread over both, the pick → next part → join flow, and the redo graph.
