How it works

Phone: press and hold on the picture to pick a colour. One finger draws, two fingers pinch, pan and turn the view (Fit straightens it). Tap with two fingers to undo, three to redo. Apple Pencil pressure changes the brush width.
Keys: Draw P, Erase E, Pick I, Fill B, Sculpt S, Smooth M, Smudge U, Mask K, Transform T, Points V, Ref R, Layers L, brush size [ ].

Colours: press and hold (or the Pick tool) takes the exact colour you see: your paint, or the drawing underneath. Unify colours turns picked colours into the nearest palette colour, shadow or highlight so everything matches, and fills small holes closed in by paint (even between layers) with the nearest colour. Layers: a layer can hold any colours. Separate colours moves the ones you choose onto their own layers. Merge colour melts every layer of the current colour into one; Merge down melts the current layer into the one below. The link button groups a layer with the one below; a group sculpts, smooths and transforms as one object. Draw: scribble with mouse or finger. Everything of one colour on a layer melts into one solid shape, so scribbling inside a circle fills it. Each layer has one colour: pick it from the palette swatches or with the Eyedropper (I, or Alt-click), and the whole layer changes. Right-click drag (or Eraser, E) erases. Sculpt: drag to push the selected layer (or group) around like liquify. Other layers never move. Shift-drag moves the whole layer. Transform: works on the selected layer (or group): drag inside the box to move. Corner squares stretch freely, or evenly with Keep proportions on (Shift swaps), side dots stretch one way, Alt scales from the centre. Rotate with the pink handle on top or by dragging just outside a corner (Shift snaps to 15°). Arrows nudge by a pixel. Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V and Ctrl+D copy, paste and duplicate it as a new layer; Flip mirrors it. Shading: each palette colour has a highlight (top) and shadow (bottom) made from the palette itself. Put shading on its own layer, give it a shadow colour, and set its Texture to dots or Fade to edge for a pixel halftone look. Smudge (U): like Smooth, but the edge also gets dragged the way you move, so you can comb a shape into a direction. Dynamic shading: set a layer's Type to Shading or Highlight. It has no colour of its own: wherever it sits over colour, that colour turns one step darker or lighter using the palette shades, and over nothing it's blank. Sculpt or smooth either layer and it updates live. Thickness: the slider in the Layers panel grows or thins the selected layer (or group) live; let go and it settles back to the middle, ready for another nudge. Mask (K) paints a soft red mask (brush size and Feather set its edge; right-click drag removes it): then Thickness only changes the masked part, fading out across the feather. Smooth: rub over a shape or line to take the kinks out. Points: the old anchor-point editing, if you want exact control. Double-click a point for a sharp corner. Fill: click an area closed in by lines on the current layer to fill it. Fills follow when you reshape. View: wheel or pinch to zoom; Space, right-drag or two fingers to pan.
